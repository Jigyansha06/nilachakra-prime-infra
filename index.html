ABOUT US

Creating spaces. Building trust.

Nilachakra Prime Infra Pvt. Ltd. is an emerging infrastructure and real-estate development company focused on creating thoughtfully planned spaces with strong fundamentals and long-term value.

Our approach goes beyond developing land and properties. We focus on location, planning, accessibility, quality and customer trust to create developments that are designed for today's needs and tomorrow's opportunities.

With a vision to contribute to the evolving infrastructure landscape of Odisha, Nilachakra Prime Infra Pvt. Ltd. aims to develop projects that combine modern planning, responsible development and lasting value.
